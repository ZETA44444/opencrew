What data we process

  • Account: e-mail, password (stored irreversibly hashed), sign-up date and the version of these terms you accepted.
  • Your content: bots, chats, memory, files, routines, watchers and activity log, stored encrypted in your separate space.
  • Keys and connections you add (API keys, app tokens): encrypted and only used for what you enable.
  • Usage: AI tokens and cost per call (to charge credits) and time using the bots' computer.
  • Technical data: IP address and sign-in attempts, for security and abuse prevention.

Why and on what basis

  • To provide the service you ask for (performance of the contract).
  • Security, abuse prevention and legal compliance (legitimate interest and legal obligation).
  • No advertising, no trackers, no data sales. The only cookie is the session one, needed to sign in.

Who it is shared with (processors)

  • Hostinger (server, in France, EU).
  • AI providers (Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, xAI, and the ones you choose with your own keys): they receive what is needed for the bot to answer. Their paid API services don't use it to train models.
  • Apps you connect (for example Composio, Zapier, GitHub, Notion): only what you ask your bots to do.
  • When they exist: the payment processor and the transactional e-mail provider.

Some AI providers are outside the EU (e.g. in the US); transfers use the safeguards provided by the GDPR (EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

How long

While you have an account. When you delete it, your space, bots and bot computers are deleted; backups roll over within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, take away (Settings → Account and data → «Download all my data») and delete your data, and object to or restrict its processing, by writing to the contact above. If we don't help you, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or your local authority.

Artificial intelligence

OpenCrew runs on AI systems: answers and files are generated by automated models. Generated images are identified as AI-made. No decisions with legal effects on you are made automatically.
