Acceptable use policy

Version 2026-11-24 · The Spanish version prevails in case of doubt.

⚠️ Draft pending legal review. The provider details will be completed before paid plans open.

Your bots act on your behalf, so this policy applies to what you do and to what you ask them to do. You must also follow the policies of the AI provider in use.

Not allowed

  • Anything illegal: fraud, scams, impersonation, harassment, hate, sexual content involving minors, weapons or malware.
  • Spam or unsolicited mass messages, or creating accounts automatically.
  • Bypassing other websites' protections or terms: if a website blocks bots or forbids automated access, the bot stops; don't try to get around it.
  • Collecting third parties' personal data without a legal basis (profiling, contact lists scraped from websites…).
  • Making other people believe they are talking to a human when they are talking to a bot.
  • Using the bots' computers to attack, scan or overload systems, mine cryptocurrency or host content.
  • Giving bots other people's passwords, card details or credentials.
  • Using the results for important decisions about people (employment, credit, health, legal) without review by a qualified person.
  • Reselling or sharing access to your account.

What happens if it's broken

We may stop a task, limit features or close the account, and cooperate with the authorities when the law requires it.

Reports

If you see misuse of OpenCrew, write to the contact shown in the terms.
